# 1. Scope and Definitions

> *"Salus populi suprema lex esto."* (The welfare of the people shall be the supreme law.)
> — Marcus Tullius Cicero, *De Legibus*

More than two thousand years ago, Cicero wrote that the good of the community should guide every law. The same idea holds in a house where dozens of families share walls, stairs and a yard. Most of the time, what is good for all of us is also good for each of us: quiet nights, clean stairwells and safe escape routes help everyone. Sometimes it means giving up a little, such as a late party or a few minutes of convenience, so that a neighbour can sleep or pass safely. Nobody is asked to forget their own needs; we only ask that, in most cases, the shared interest comes first. If we treat each other with patience and good will, we can live together in harmony, as good neighbours and agreeable people.

These rules apply to buildings A, B and C around the shared inner yard. A **resident** is anyone who lives in one of the buildings: tenants, owners and their household members. The **house management** administers the buildings and enforces these rules. Every resident must follow them.
