**What pass 2 does.** One call per ingestion. It gets every term with its mentions and the text around them, plus all concepts, both new and already in the ontology. It answers with two things:
- **Synonym groups** merge new concepts that are the same thing. The merged concept's evidence and parents go to the kept one, and relations, rules, properties and term mentions are re-pointed to it.
- **Sense splits** assign each mention of a homonym to the concept it names in that place, with a scope note per sense.

Terms with the same label become one term record. A concept already in the ontology is never merged away or changed; new concepts can merge into it. Invalid answer items get one retry, and anything that still fails or cites an unknown alias is reported in the run record and left out, as in pass 1.
